# Polymarket paper desk

A read-only **paper trading** dashboard for six fake-money Polymarket books: the fast crypto bot (BTC, ETH, SOL accounts) and three research desks (Crypto, Sports, Politics). Nothing here is a real bet; no wallet is connected, and the page cannot place, close, or change any trade.

Live page: https://xenokynbot.github.io/poly-paper-dashboard/

Snapshots are published to the `data` branch about every minute; the page refreshes about every 30 seconds and lags the books by one to two minutes. All times ET.
